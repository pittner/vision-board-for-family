# Family Vision Board 2027

36 family goals in 6 groups (time together, home, health and outdoors, learning and growing, travel and memories, kindness and traditions). Each goal has why it matters and a first step the whole family can do this week, plus 12 questions kids can answer and a 45-minute plan for one evening over the holidays.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-for-family/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=family_2027) with the first step under it; save it as a 1080x1920 lock screen or print it for the fridge
- Or type your own family goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
